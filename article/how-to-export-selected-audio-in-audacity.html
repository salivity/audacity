How to Export Selected Audio in Audacity

Audacity allows you to save a specific portion of your project rather than the entire timeline. This guide provides a straightforward, step-by-step walkthrough on how to highlight a specific audio region and export only that selection into your preferred audio format, such as MP3, WAV, or AAC.

Step 1: Select the Audio Region

  1. Open your project in Audacity.
  2. Select the Selection Tool from the toolbar (or press F1).
  3. Click and drag your cursor over the specific section of the audio track you wish to save. The selected area will appear highlighted with a darker background.
  4. Press the Spacebar to playback and confirm that you have selected the exact section you need.

Step 2: Open the Export Menu

Depending on the version of Audacity you are using, the export command differs slightly:

  • Audacity 3.4 and Newer:
    1. Click File in the top menu bar.
    2. Navigate to Export Audio...
    3. In the export dialog window, look for the Export Range section and select the radio button for Current Selection.
  • Audacity 3.3 and Older:
    1. Click File in the top menu bar.
    2. Hover over Export.
    3. Click Export Selected Audio...

Step 3: Configure Your Audio Settings

  1. File Name: Enter a name for your exported file.
  2. Folder: Choose the destination folder on your computer where the file will be saved.
  3. Format: Choose your desired file format (e.g., MP3, WAV, FLAC, or Ogg Vorbis) from the format dropdown menu.
  4. Format Options: Adjust quality settings such as sample rate, bit depth, or encoding mode according to your needs.

Step 4: Finalize and Export

  1. Click the Export (or Save) button.
  2. If prompted, edit the Metadata Tags (such as Artist Name, Track Title, and Album Title) or leave them blank.
  3. Click OK.

Audacity will render and save only the highlighted timeline region to your chosen destination.
