How to Mute All Other Tracks in Audacity

Audacity allows you to instantly silence every track in your project except the one you need to focus on by using the Solo function. Rather than manually clicking the Mute button on dozens of individual audio channels, you can isolate a single track with a single click or keyboard shortcut. This guide covers how to use the Solo button, configure Audacity's solo preferences, and use keyboard shortcuts to streamline your audio editing workflow.

Using the Solo Button

The fastest way to isolate a single track is using the Track Control Panel:

  1. Locate the Track Control Panel on the far left side of the track you want to hear.
  2. Click the Solo button (located directly beneath the track name and next to the Mute button).
  3. Audacity will automatically mute all other tracks, graying out their waveforms and highlighting the active Solo button.
  4. To unmute the other tracks and return to normal playback, click the Solo button again.

Using Keyboard Shortcuts

If you prefer navigating via the keyboard:

  1. Use the Up and Down arrow keys to move focus to the track you want to isolate.
  2. Press Shift + S to toggle the Solo state on the selected track.
  3. Press Shift + S again to deactivate Solo and restore playback to all tracks.

Configuring Solo Behavior in Preferences

Audacity offers different Solo modes depending on how you prefer to work with multiple tracks:

  1. Open the preferences menu by going to Edit > Preferences on Windows/Linux, or Audacity > Preferences on macOS.
  2. Click on Tracks Behaviors in the left sidebar.
  3. Locate the Solo button setting, which provides three options:
    • Simple (Default): Clicking Solo on one track mutes all others. Clicking Solo on a different track deselects the first and solos the new one.
    • Multi-track: Allows you to solo more than one track at a time without unsoloing the previous ones.
    • None: Removes the Solo button entirely, requiring you to manually manage the Mute buttons.
  4. Select your preferred mode and click OK to save the changes.
