How to Float Audacity Toolbars as Windows

Audacity allows users to fully customize their workspace by detaching toolbars from the main application frame and floating them as independent windows. This guide explains how to undock toolbars, reposition them across single or multi-monitor setups, and snap them back into the default layout when needed.

Yes, toolbars in Audacity can be floated as standalone independent windows. Nearly every toolbar in the interface—including the Transport, Tools, Edit, Recording Meter, Playback Meter, Device, and Selection toolbars—can be detached from the main interface dock.

How to Float a Toolbar

  1. Locate the toolbar you want to detach.
  2. Find the grab handle on the far left edge of the toolbar, represented by a vertical textured or serrated strip.
  3. Click and hold the grab handle with your left mouse button.
  4. Drag the toolbar away from the top or bottom docking panel into the center of the screen or onto an adjacent monitor.
  5. Release the mouse button.

Once released, the toolbar turns into an independent floating window complete with its own title bar and close button. You can resize certain floating toolbars, such as the Recording and Playback Meters, by dragging their edges to provide larger, more visible visual feedback during sessions.

How to Re-dock or Reset Toolbars

To return a floating toolbar to the main window:

  • Manual Re-docking: Click and drag the title bar of the floating window back toward the upper or lower docking areas of the main Audacity window until a blue placeholder highlight appears, then release the mouse.
  • Double-Clicking: Double-clicking the title bar of a floating toolbar will instantly return it to its previous docked position.
  • Resetting the Layout: If windows become lost or disorganized, navigate to the top menu and select View > Toolbars > Reset Toolbars. This immediately returns all toolbars to their default docked positions.
