Maximum Bit Depth for Recording in Audacity

This article outlines the maximum bit depth supported for recording raw audio in Audacity, explains the differences between software capabilities and hardware limitations, and details how to configure your project settings for the highest possible audio fidelity.

The Maximum Bit Depth in Audacity

The maximum bit depth supported for recording and processing raw audio in Audacity is 32-bit float.

Audacity natively supports three primary sample formats:

  • 16-bit: The standard resolution for CD-quality audio, offering 96 dB of dynamic range.
  • 24-bit: The professional recording studio standard, offering 144 dB of dynamic range.
  • 32-bit float: The maximum resolution in Audacity, providing a theoretical dynamic range exceeding 1,500 dB.

32-Bit Float vs. Hardware Capabilities

While Audacity can record directly to a 32-bit float format, your actual capture quality is bound by your hardware. Most standard audio interfaces and analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) operate at a maximum hardware depth of 24-bit. When you record a 24-bit interface into a 32-bit float track, Audacity pads the samples to 32-bit precision.

However, if you use modern 32-bit float audio recorders or interfaces (such as specific field recorders from Zoom, Sound Devices, or modern USB microphones), Audacity can capture genuine 32-bit float raw audio without hardware-level digital clipping.

Advantages of 32-Bit Float Recording

Recording at 32-bit float provides significant processing advantages:

  • Immunity to Internal Clipping: In 32-bit float, audio signals that exceed 0 dBFS (decibels full scale) do not clip or distort permanently. If a signal peaks above 0 dB, you can simply reduce the gain or apply normalization later to recover the waveform without loss of data.
  • Lower Noise Floor During Processing: Applying multiple digital signal processing (DSP) operations—such as equalization, compression, or reverb—introduces quantization noise. Processing in 32-bit float minimizes rounding errors across complex signal chains.

How to Set Maximum Bit Depth in Audacity

To ensure Audacity records at its maximum bit depth:

  1. Open Audacity and navigate to Edit (Windows/Linux) or Audacity (macOS).
  2. Select Preferences.
  3. Choose Audio Settings (or Quality in older versions) from the sidebar.
  4. Locate the Default Sample Format dropdown.
  5. Select 32-bit float and click OK.

Any new audio tracks created or recorded will now default to Audacity's maximum supported 32-bit float resolution.
