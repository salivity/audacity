How to Use the Selection Tool in Audacity

This guide explains how to activate and use the Selection Tool in Audacity to highlight a specific audio region. You will learn how to choose the tool, select an area using mouse controls and keyboard shortcuts, and adjust the selection boundaries with precision for editing, deleting, or applying effects.

Activating the Selection Tool

The Selection Tool is the default cursor tool in Audacity. If it is not active:

  1. Locate the Tools Toolbar (usually near the top-left area of the Audacity interface).
  2. Click the Selection Tool icon, which resembles an uppercase "I" (the standard text I-beam cursor).
  3. Alternatively, press the shortcut key F1 on your keyboard to instantly switch to the Selection Tool.

Highlighting an Audio Region by Clicking and Dragging

  1. Move your cursor over the audio track waveform where you want your selection to begin.
  2. Left-click and hold at the desired starting point.
  3. Drag the cursor horizontally across the waveform to the desired end point.
  4. Release the mouse button. The selected region will appear highlighted in a darker color relative to the rest of the track.

Fine-Tuning the Selection Edges

If the initial highlight is not exact, you can adjust either boundary without redoing the entire selection:

  1. Hover your cursor near the left or right edge of the highlighted area.
  2. The cursor icon will transform into a white hand pointing left or right.
  3. Click and drag the boundary inward to shrink the selection or outward to expand it.

Alternative Precision Selection Methods

  • Shift-Click Method: Click on the waveform to place your cursor at the start position, then hold down the Shift key and left-click at the end position. Audacity will automatically highlight the area between the two clicks.
  • Selection Toolbar (Numeric Entry): At the bottom of the Audacity workspace, locate the Selection Toolbar. Here, you can manually type exact numeric time values (hours, minutes, seconds, milliseconds) for the Start and End or Length of your desired audio region.
  • Multi-Track Selection: To apply the exact same time selection across multiple tracks, click and drag diagonally up or down across the tracks while highlighting horizontally.
