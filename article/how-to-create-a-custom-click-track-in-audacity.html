How to Create a Custom Click Track in Audacity

Generating a custom click track in Audacity is a straightforward process that provides an accurate rhythmic reference for recording music, podcasts, or voiceovers. This guide demonstrates how to use Audacity’s built-in Rhythm Track generator to configure your exact beats per minute (BPM), customize beats per bar to match your time signature, and set the appropriate duration for your project.

Step 1: Open the Rhythm Track Generator

  1. Launch Audacity and open a new or existing project.
  2. In the top menu bar, click on Generate.
  3. Select Rhythm Track... from the drop-down menu (in older versions of Audacity, this option is labeled as Click Track).

Step 2: Set Your Custom Tempo and Time Signature

The Rhythm Track dialog box contains several parameters to customize your timing:

  • Tempo [beats per minute]: Enter your desired BPM directly into the field (e.g., 120 for standard pop/rock tempos, or any custom value between 30 and 300).
  • Beats per bar: Enter the top number of your time signature. For example, enter 4 for 4/4 time, 3 for 3/4 time, or 6 for 6/8 time.
  • Swing amount: Leave this at 0 for straight time, or adjust the slider to add a swing groove.

Step 3: Configure Track Length and Click Sounds

Before generating the track, customize how it sounds and how long it plays:

  • Rhythm track duration: You can define track length either by entering the total Number of bars or by switching the mode to specify a exact duration in minutes and seconds.
  • Click sound: Choose your preferred sound profile from the drop-down menu, such as "Ping," "Beep," "Metronome," or "Cowbell."
  • High/Low click frequencies: The first beat of each bar uses a higher frequency to signal the downbeat, while subsequent beats use a lower frequency. You can adjust these pitch values in Hertz if needed.

Step 4: Generate the Track

  1. Click the Preview button to hear a short sample of your settings.
  2. If satisfied, click Apply (or OK).

Audacity will create a new mono audio track containing your customized metronome. You can adjust its volume slider on the left-hand track panel so it sits at a comfortable monitoring level while you record.
