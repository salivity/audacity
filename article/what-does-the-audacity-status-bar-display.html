What Does the Audacity Status Bar Display?

The Audacity status bar, located at the very bottom of the main application window, provides essential real-time feedback about your project, current operations, and system resources. It serves as a dynamic information center that updates automatically as you interact with different tools, perform playback, or record audio. This guide details every component and piece of information displayed on the status bar to help you navigate your audio editing workflow efficiently.

Contextual Hints and Tool Messages

The left side of the status bar dynamically displays context-sensitive help messages and instructions based on the current position of your mouse cursor:

  • Tool Tips: Hovering over any button, slider, or menu item reveals a short description of its function.
  • Action Prompts: When hovering over the track canvas or waveform, the bar provides instructions on how to use the active tool (for example, "Click and drag to select audio").
  • Keyboard Modifier Hints: The status bar frequently shows how holding modifier keys (such as Shift, Ctrl, or Alt/Option) changes the behavior of the current tool, such as enabling fine-tuning, zooming, or alternate selection modes.

Transport and Playback State

The status bar reports the current transport activity of the software. It explicitly states whether Audacity is:

  • Stopped: The default state when no playback or recording is active.
  • Playing: Active during audio playback.
  • Recording: Active when capturing audio, often accompanied by the duration recorded.
  • Paused: Indicates that playback or recording has been temporarily halted.

During heavy processing tasks—such as rendering, applying effects, or importing large files—the status bar often displays progress text and status updates regarding the active operation.

Disk Space and Recording Time Remaining

On the right side of the status bar, Audacity calculates and displays the amount of recording time remaining based on available hard drive space:

  • Time Calculation: The readout (e.g., "Disk space remains for recording 24 hours and 12 minutes") continuously estimates available recording time.
  • Dynamic Adjustment: This value automatically updates if you change the project sample rate, switch between mono and stereo recording channels, or change the default sample format (bit depth), as these parameters directly alter the data rate per second.

Distinguishing the Status Bar from Bottom Toolbars

By default, Audacity docks the Selection Toolbar and Time Toolbar directly above or adjacent to the status bar at the bottom of the interface. While visually close, these toolbars provide complementary numeric data rather than status messages:

  • Project Sample Rate: The active sampling frequency (such as 44,100 Hz or 48,000 Hz).
  • Time Readouts: Exact values for selection start, selection end, project length, and playback cursor position.
  • Snap-To Settings: Indicators showing whether cursor movements snap to specific time boundaries or musical beats.
