How to Change Themes in Audacity

Audacity offers built-in visual customization options allowing users to switch between Light, Dark, High Contrast, and Classic themes to suit their workflow and reduce eye strain. This guide provides step-by-step instructions on accessing the interface settings and applying your preferred theme without needing any external plugins or modifications.

Step 1: Open the Preferences Menu

To change the theme, you must first access the Audacity Preferences window:

  • Windows and Linux: Click on Edit in the top menu bar and select Preferences, or press Ctrl + P on your keyboard.
  • macOS: Click on Audacity in the top menu bar and select Preferences, or press Cmd + , on your keyboard.

Step 2: Navigate to Interface Settings

  1. In the left-hand sidebar of the Preferences window, locate and click on the Interface category.
  2. Locate the Display section within the Interface settings panel on the right.
  3. Find the Theme dropdown menu.

Step 3: Choose Your Theme

Click the Theme dropdown menu to select from the four available options:

  • Light: The standard modern interface featuring bright backgrounds and clear, contemporary icons.
  • Dark: An inverted color scheme designed for low-light environments to reduce visual fatigue during long editing sessions.
  • High Contrast: A stark, two-tone theme with maximized color differentiation to improve accessibility and readability.
  • Classic: The legacy aesthetic reminiscent of older Audacity versions, featuring the traditional gray layout and original toolbar icons.

Step 4: Apply the Changes

Once you select your desired theme, click the OK button at the bottom of the Preferences window. Audacity will instantly apply the new visual style across the entire workspace without requiring a software restart.
