What Does Rescan Audio Devices Do in Audacity?

The "Rescan Audio Devices" command in Audacity refreshes the software's internal list of available playback and recording hardware without requiring you to restart the program. This article explains how this function works, why it is an essential troubleshooting tool for audio production, and how to use it when connecting new microphones, interfaces, or headphones during an active session.

The Function of Rescan Audio Devices

By default, Audacity queries your computer’s operating system for connected audio hardware only once: when the application first boots up. If you plug in a USB microphone, connect an audio interface, or pair Bluetooth headphones after Audacity is already running, the software will not automatically detect these new peripherals.

The "Rescan Audio Devices" command forces Audacity to communicate with your operating system’s audio driver system immediately. It checks for any changes in the hardware configuration and updates the device lists displayed in your Recording Device and Playback Device dropdown menus.

Why This Tool Is Important

Before this feature was introduced, users who connected a new device mid-session had to save their project, close Audacity, and reopen it to access their hardware. The Rescan Audio Devices tool streamlines this process by:

  • Saving Time: Eliminates the need to restart the program, keeping your workflow uninterrupted.
  • Preserving System Resources: Prevents the disruption of active editing sessions or complex, unsaved multi-track layouts.
  • Fixing Initialization Errors: Clears common software errors, such as "Error opening recording device," which often occur when a previously selected device is unplugged or enters sleep mode.

How to Use the Command

  1. Connect or disconnect your audio hardware (e.g., plug in your USB microphone).
  2. Click on the Transport menu in the top menu bar.
  3. Select Rescan Audio Devices.
  4. Check your Audio Setup toolbar (or Device Toolbar in older versions). Your new input and output devices will now be selectable in the respective dropdown menus.
