How to Zoom Horizontally and Vertically in Audacity

Navigating audio waveforms accurately in Audacity requires mastering both horizontal zooming (adjusting the time scale) and vertical zooming (adjusting the amplitude or track height). This guide provides direct, step-by-step instructions for using mouse shortcuts, keyboard commands, and interface tools to zoom in and out across both axes quickly.

Horizontal Zooming (Time Scale)

Horizontal zooming expands or compresses the timeline, allowing you to inspect individual audio samples or view the entire project at once.

Using Mouse and Scroll Wheel Shortcuts

  • Zoom In: Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on macOS) and scroll the mouse wheel up.
  • Zoom Out: Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on macOS) and scroll the mouse wheel down.
  • Timeline Ruler Click: Hover the mouse cursor over the Timeline Ruler at the top of the project. Left-click to zoom in at that point, or right-click (or hold Shift and left-click) to zoom out.

Using Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Zoom In: Press Ctrl + 1 (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + 1 (macOS).
  • Zoom Normal (Default View): Press Ctrl + 2 (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + 2 (macOS).
  • Zoom Out: Press Ctrl + 3 (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + 3 (macOS).
  • Fit Project to Window: Press Ctrl + F (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + F (macOS).
  • Zoom to Selection: Press Ctrl + E (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + E (macOS) to expand only the highlighted audio selection across the entire screen.

Using the Zoom Toolbar

  • Click the Magnifying Glass (+) icon in the Edit Toolbar to zoom in.
  • Click the Magnifying Glass (-) icon to zoom out.
  • Use the Fit Selection and Fit Project buttons located next to the zoom icons for quick framing.

Vertical Zooming (Amplitude and Track Height)

Vertical zooming changes the visual representation of volume levels (amplitude) or expands the physical height of the track lane on the screen.

Zooming the Vertical Scale (Amplitude)

The Vertical Scale ruler is located on the far-left edge of the waveform, displaying values from -1.0 to +1.0 (linear) or decibels (dB).

  • Zoom In: Hover your cursor over the Vertical Scale ruler until the cursor turns into a magnifying glass. Left-click to zoom in vertically toward the center line.
  • Zoom Out: With the magnifying glass cursor active on the ruler, right-click (or hold Shift and left-click) to zoom out.
  • Reset Vertical Zoom: Right-click anywhere on the Vertical Scale ruler and select Reset Zoom from the context menu (or middle-click the scale).

Adjusting Physical Track Height

  • Manual Dragging: Move your mouse cursor over the bottom border of the track control panel (on the far left). When the cursor changes to an up-and-down arrow, click and drag downward to increase track height or upward to decrease it.
  • Fit All Tracks Vertically: Press Ctrl + Shift + F (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + F (macOS) to automatically resize all visible tracks to fit within the current window height.
  • Collapse/Expand Track: Click the upward-pointing triangle icon at the bottom-left corner of the track control panel to quickly collapse the track, and click it again to expand it back to full size.
