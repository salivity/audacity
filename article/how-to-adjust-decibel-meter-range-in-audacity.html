How to Adjust Decibel Meter Range in Audacity

Adjusting the decibel (dB) meter display range in Audacity allows you to monitor quieter signals with greater precision or narrow the visual dynamic range for standard mixing. By default, Audacity displays a standard range, but this can be customized to show anywhere from -36 dB down to -145 dB depending on your project needs. This guide details the exact steps to locate and change the dB meter display settings within Audacity’s preferences.

Adjusting the Meter Range via Preferences

  1. Open the Preferences Menu:
    • On Windows and Linux, click Edit in the top menu bar and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
    • On macOS, click Audacity in the menu bar and select Preferences (or press Cmd + ,).
  2. Navigate to Interface Settings:
    • In the left-hand navigation pane of the Preferences window, click on the Interface category.
  3. Modify the dB Range:
    • Look for the dropdown menu labeled Meter/Waveform dB range.
    • Click the dropdown to view available display ranges (e.g., -36 dB, -60 dB, -96 dB, or -145 dB).
    • Select the range that best suits your project:
      • -60 dB: Suitable for voiceover, podcasting, and standard broadcast monitoring.
      • -96 dB: Ideal for 16-bit audio mastering, capturing finer details in noise floors and reverb tails.
      • -145 dB: Best for high-resolution 24-bit audio analysis and precision noise floor detection.
  4. Save Your Changes:
    • Click the OK button at the bottom right to apply the new settings. The recording and playback meters will automatically update to reflect the new dynamic range.

Alternative Quick Method

You can also access meter settings directly from the main interface:

  1. Right-click on either the Recording Meter or Playback Meter toolbar.
  2. Select Options... from the context menu.
  3. Choose your preferred range directly from the settings dialog and click OK.
