How to Set Up Pre-Roll in Audacity

Punch-in recording, officially known as Punch and Roll in Audacity, allows you to correct mistakes by seamlessly re-recording over a specific section of an audio track. Setting up a pre-roll duration provides a playback buffer of your existing audio right before the recording triggers, giving you time to match your tone, rhythm, and pacing. This guide outlines the direct steps to configure and customize your pre-roll duration within Audacity’s preferences.

Adjusting the Pre-Roll Duration

  1. Open Preferences:
    • Windows/Linux: Go to the menu bar, click Edit, and select Preferences (or press Ctrl + P).
    • macOS: Click Audacity in the application menu and select Preferences (or press Cmd + ,).
  2. Navigate to Recording Settings:
    • In the left sidebar of the Preferences window, click on Recording.
  3. Set the Pre-roll Value:
    • Locate the Punch and Roll Recording section.
    • Find the Pre-roll input box (measured in seconds).
    • Enter your preferred duration. The default is typically set to 5.0 seconds, but 3.0 to 5.0 seconds is ideal for voiceover work to properly catch the cadence.
    • (Optional) You can also adjust the Crossfade duration in the same section (measured in milliseconds) to ensure the splice point blends smoothly without clicks.
  4. Save Changes:
    • Click OK at the bottom right to apply and save your settings.

Performing a Punch and Roll Recording

Once your pre-roll time is set:

  1. Click the timeline where you want to replace audio (just before the error).
  2. Press Shift + D or navigate to Transport > Recording > Punch and Roll Record.
  3. Audacity will automatically play back the audio for your designated pre-roll duration. Once the playhead hits the cursor position, it will automatically switch to recording mode, replacing the subsequent audio.
